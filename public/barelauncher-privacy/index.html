Privacy Policy for Bare Launcher

Effective Date: October 8, 2026 | Last Updated: October 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Bare Launcher ("we", "our", or "the App"), developed by Arron McCrory, handles information when you install and use the application on your Android device.

We are committed to maintaining your privacy. Bare Launcher is designed from the ground up as a minimalist, distraction-free home launcher that respects user privacy and data ownership.

1. Information We Collect

Bare Launcher does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal information or sensitive user data.

Local Device Information & Permissions

To function as an Android home screen launcher, the App accesses certain device capabilities strictly on your local device:

  • Installed Applications (QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES): The App reads the list of applications installed on your device solely to display them in your App Drawer, search index, and Home Screen shortcuts. This application data is processed purely in real-time on your device and is never transmitted, uploaded, or shared with anyone.
  • Local Usage & Preferences (Stored On-Device Only): The App locally tracks launch frequency (click counts) to calculate your "Frequent Apps" list, and stores your customization choices (such as active color themes, custom shortcut slots, app label visibility, and hidden apps list). All of this data is stored strictly within private, local application storage (SharedPreferences) on your device. It never leaves your device and is deleted if you clear app data or uninstall the App.

2. Third-Party Services and Analytics

  • No Third-Party SDKs: Bare Launcher does not integrate any third-party software development kits (SDKs), advertising networks, or user tracking frameworks.
  • No Analytics: We do not use Google Analytics, Firebase, Facebook SDK, or any crash reporting tools that collect telemetry or device identifiers.
  • No Advertisements: Bare Launcher is completely ad-free and does not use Google's Advertising ID (AD_ID).

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

Because we do not collect any personal data, we do not sell, rent, trade, or share user data with any third parties under any circumstances.

4. Children's Privacy

Bare Launcher does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. It is safe for users of all ages.

5. Security

We follow the principle of data minimization: we do not collect your data in the first place. Any configuration settings or launch counts remain securely protected by the Android operating system within your device's private storage sandbox.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
